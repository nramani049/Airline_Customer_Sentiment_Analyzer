ℹ️ Methodology, VADER Thresholds & Limitations ▼
VADER Sentiment Classification Methodology

Engine: VADER (Valence Aware Dictionary and sEntiment Reasoner) polarity analyzer.

Compound Score: Normalized metric ranging from -1.0 (Extremely Negative) to +1.0 (Extremely Positive).

  • Positive: Compound Score ≥ +0.05
  • Negative: Compound Score ≤ -0.05
  • Neutral: -0.05 < Compound Score < +0.05
Practical Limitations & Considerations

VADER is a rule-based lexicon model, not a large language model. It can occasionally misclassify sarcasm or aviation-specific abbreviations. Reviews are synthetic demonstration records spanning 2020 through 2026.
